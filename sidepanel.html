What am I looking at?

Every time a web page loads, your browser quietly makes many separate requests: the page itself, images, fonts, scripts, and calls to other companies' servers (ads, analytics, social buttons). Each row here is one of those requests.

  • Outgoing: the request your browser sends (the address and method).
  • Incoming: the response that comes back (the result code, size and content type).
  • Third-party: the request went to a different site than the page that asked for it. These are often ads, trackers, or content delivery networks.
  • Sites tab: groups requests by site and explains what each one is probably for (ads, analytics, fonts, content delivery…). "Known service" comes from a built-in list; "Best guess" is inferred from the address and traffic, so treat it as a hint.
  • Tracking ping: a small beacon sent mainly to report that something happened, such as a page view or a click.

This extension only watches. It never blocks, changes or sends your data anywhere. The log stays in your browser and is erased when you close the browser or press Clear.

Request and response contents (the body) are not visible to extensions, so only addresses, headers-derived info and results are shown.
